# Buddy action polish

Job: find a Buddy and take the next useful action without mistaking controls for
body text. Ease and agency are the relevant behavioural lenses. Existing routes,
school pairings, community consent, messages and data contracts stay unchanged.

Primary actions retain the shared textured matcha style. Secondary actions get
ivory surfaces, borders, soft shadows and at least 44px touch targets. Change year,
Refresh, back, Open and profile disclosures now have visible surfaces. Tabs have a
clear selected state. Mobile recommendation actions share a full-width row.
Profile editing is labelled “Edit buddy profile”; the explanation of anonymous
visibility stays inside. Preview calls to action look clickable without nesting
another button inside the card button. Shared Button now preserves its base class
when callers provide their own class and merges custom styles.

Hierarchy: next action, navigation, optional details. Existing loading, empty,
success, error and disabled states remain. No new notifications, fake reputation
or attention rewards. Success is successful Buddy connections and resolved
requests; it is not clicks or time on page. No SQL or production data changes.

Review first-year and upper-year views, Change year and Cancel, My Buddy cards,
community post preview, invitations, expandable profiles and narrow viewports.
Changes are scoped to Buddy so Give & Ask and Practice Together remain unaffected.
